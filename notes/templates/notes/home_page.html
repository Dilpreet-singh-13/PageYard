{# Feature 1 #}

Pages & Notes

Write in Markdown with full syntax support — headings, lists, code blocks, tables, math (KaTeX), and embedded images.

{# Feature 2 #}

Smart Tags

Organize your knowledge with a flexible tagging system. Create, search, and filter by tags to find exactly what you need.

{# Feature 3 #}

Instant Search

Full-text search powered by PostgreSQL. Filter by date range, tags, starred status — find any note in milliseconds.

Trash & Recovery

Accidentally deleted something? Notes stay in your trash for 30 days. Restore with a single click.
